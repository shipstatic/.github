## Deploy static websites, landing pages, and prototypes instantly. Free, no account needed.

Made something with AI and want it online? [ShipStatic](https://www.shipstatic.com) gives you a real link you can share, in seconds.

## Try it in seconds, no account needed

**With your AI agent.** Drop `https://mcp.shipstatic.com` into any MCP client, then ask your agent to put your site online.

**From the terminal.** Run one command and point it at the folder your site is built into:

```bash
npx @shipstatic/ship ./dist
```

There's nothing to install and nothing to sign up for. You get back a link you can share right away, and a claim link. The site stays up for 3 days, and opening the claim link keeps it for good.

Want it private? Add a password, and visitors have to type it before they see anything:

```bash
npx @shipstatic/ship ./dist --password "pick-a-password"
```

## Keep your sites with a free account

Everything above works with no account. A free account keeps your sites for good, lets you use your own domain, and shows you everything you've published in one place. Sign up at [my.shipstatic.com](https://my.shipstatic.com).
